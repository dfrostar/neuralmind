# About the Authors

**Darren Frost** has spent his career at the intersection of healthcare and technology — building the systems that make clinical workflows, regulatory compliance, and patient-facing tools actually work. He's not a doctor, but he's spent years reading clinical literature because he believes that clear, honest communication about medical evidence is a form of patient care in its own right. This book grew out of a simple observation: patients considering peptide therapy are drowning in marketing and starving for facts. Darren lives in Philadelphia, where he is an active angel investor in healthcare technology startups and a frequent contributor to open-source medical software projects.

**Rye Estepp, MD** is a board-certified physician practicing peptide medicine, regenerative medicine, and functional medicine at Meeting Point Health in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Over the past five years, he has treated thousands of patients with both FDA-approved and compounded peptide therapies, managing outcomes across a spectrum that runs from athletic recovery to metabolic health to anti-aging applications. He is a recurring lecturer at the Peptide World Congress and has contributed to the growing body of clinical literature on peptide therapeutics. Dr. Estepp did not set out to specialize in peptides — he followed the evidence. What he found was a class of molecules with remarkable therapeutic potential operating inside a regulatory framework that has not caught up to the science.

*Why two authors?* Peer-reviewed research is one kind of evidence. Clinical experience with thousands of real patients is another. Neither is complete on its own. Darren brings the research literacy and the patient perspective; Rye brings the clinical judgment and the frontline experience. Between us, we hope to give you a picture that is both scientifically rigorous and practically useful — a map of the landscape that shows not just where the roads are paved but where they turn to dirt.
