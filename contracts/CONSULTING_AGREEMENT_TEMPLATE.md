## 2. SCOPE OF WORK

Consultant will provide CFO Advisory services across three phases:

### Phase 1 — Assessment
Consultant will conduct a technical and strategic assessment of Client's codebase and development practices, evaluating readiness for AI-augmented development workflows.

### Phase 2 — Deploy
Consultant will assist in deploying NeuralMind infrastructure, including graph-based semantic code intelligence integration with Client's development environment, tooling configuration, and team onboarding.

### Phase 3 — Measure
Consultant will track deployment outcomes, measure token efficiency and code comprehension benchmarks, and deliver performance analysis against agreed KPIs.

---

## 3. DELIVERABLES

The following deliverables will be produced across the engagement phases:

| Deliverable | Description | Phase |
|-------------|-------------|-------|
| **Codebase Audit Report** | Technical assessment of Client's repository structure, code quality metrics, and integration readiness | Assessment |
| **NeuralMind Deployment** | Fully configured NeuralMind instance integrated with Client's toolchain(s), with installation verification | Deploy |
| **Benchmark Report** | Quantitative analysis of token consumption, context retrieval accuracy, and code comprehension improvements before vs. after deployment | Measure |
| **Executive Readout** | Presentation to Client leadership summarizing findings, deployment status, and recommended next steps | Measure |

## 4. COMPENSATION

Fees are fixed per engagement tier, inclusive of Consultant's labor materials, travel (if applicable), and expenses unless otherwise agreed in writing.

| Tier | Fee | Description |
|------|-----|-------------|
| **Assessment** | **$35,000 USD** | Codebase audit and readiness evaluation only |
| **Pilot** | **$55,000 USD** | Assessment + limited-scope NeuralMind deployment (one team or one repository) |
| **Enterprise** | **$85,000 USD / year** | Full deployment across Client organization with ongoing support, benchmarking, and quarterly reviews |

Client and Consultant will agree to a specific Engagement Tier in a Statement of Work ("SOW") executed under this Agreement. Each SOW will reference this Agreement and specify the applicable Tier, fee, timeline, and any scope adjustments.

## 5. PAYMENT TERMS

- **50% upfront** upon execution of the applicable SOW
- **50% on completion**, defined as delivery of all deliverables for the applicable Engagement Tier and written acceptance by Client (or 10 days after delivery without written rejection specifying material nonconformance)

Invoices are due net 30 days from receipt. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of the other's proprietary information ("Confidential Information") disclosed during the engagement. Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

- Client's source code, architecture, financial data, and business metrics
- Benchmark results, performance data, and assessment findings
- Any information marked or reasonably understood to be confidential

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is publicly known through no breach, (b) was rightfully possessed prior to disclosure, (c) is independently developed without use of Confidential Information, or (d) is required to be disclosed by law (with prompt notice to the disclosing party).

This confidentiality obligation survives termination of this Agreement for a period of **three (3) years**.

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

**Initial Term:** This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues for **one (1) year**, automatically renewing for successive one-year periods unless either party provides 30 days' written notice of non-renewal.

**Termination for Convenience:** Either party may terminate an active SOW with 30 days' written notice.

**Kill Fee:** If Client terminates an SOW early without cause, Client shall pay a kill fee equal to 50% of the remaining unbilled fees for the applicable Engagement Tier, unless otherwise specified in the SOW.

**Effect of Termination:** Upon termination, Consultant will deliver all completed or in-progress deliverables. Client will pay for all work performed and expenses incurred through the termination date.
