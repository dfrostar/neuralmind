## 6. What NOT to say

- "AI-powered" — meaningless to CTOs and CFOs
- "Zero friction" — there's always friction; "reduced friction" is honest
- "Self-improving product" — Tier D until measured fitness gain in production
- "SOC 2 compliant" — architecture supports, certification Q3 2027
- "Enterprise-ready" unless you can point to a live customer with permission
- Any dollar figure not traced to BUSINESS-CASE.md or `neuralmind savings --cost`
