# NeuralMind Trademark Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-30

---

## 1. Purpose

This Trademark Policy protects the NeuralMind brand and ensures consistent use of the project's trademarks. It provides guidelines for using the NeuralMind name and logo in projects, products, and services.

---

## 2. Trademarks

The following are trademarks or registered trademarks of the NeuralMind project:

- **NeuralMind** (word mark)
- **NeuralMind logo** (logo mark)
- **NM** (abbreviation, when used in connection with NeuralMind products)

---

## 3. Permitted Uses

You may use the NeuralMind trademarks without prior permission for the following purposes:

### 3.1 Referential Use
- **Academic papers, articles, and blog posts** that reference NeuralMind
- **Documentation and tutorials** that describe how to use NeuralMind
- **Conference presentations and talks** about NeuralMind

### 3.2 Community Use
- **Meetup groups and user groups** that focus on NeuralMind (e.g., "NeuralMind User Group of Austin")
- **Open-source projects** that integrate with NeuralMind (e.g., "NeuralMind Integration for VS Code")
- **Social media accounts** that discuss NeuralMind (must include "fan" or "community" in the name)

### 3.3 Compatibility Claims
- **Products that work with NeuralMind** may state compatibility (e.g., "Works with NeuralMind")
- **Plugins and extensions** may use "NeuralMind" to describe compatibility

---

## 4. Prohibited Uses

You may NOT use the NeuralMind trademarks for the following without explicit written permission:

### 4.1 Product Names
- **Competing products** may not use "NeuralMind" in their name
- **Derivative works** may not be called "NeuralMind [Something]" unless officially recognized
- **Rebranded forks** must use a different name

### 4.2 Domain Names
- **NeuralMind-related domains** (e.g., neuralmind-tools.com, buy-neuralmind.com) require permission
- **Fan sites** must include "fan" or "community" in the domain

### 4.3 Logos and Branding
- **Modified logos** require permission
- **Merchandise** (t-shirts, stickers, etc.) requires permission for commercial distribution

---

## 5. Requirements for Permitted Uses

When using NeuralMind trademarks under Section 3, you must:

1. **Include a trademark notice**: "NeuralMind is a trademark of the NeuralMind project."
2. **Use the trademark correctly**: "NeuralMind" (not "NEURALMIND" or "neuralmind" unless context requires lowercase)
3. **Not imply endorsement**: Your use must not suggest that the NeuralMind project endorses or sponsors your product/service
4. **Maintain quality standards**: Products or services associated with the NeuralMind name must meet reasonable quality standards

---

## 6. Enforcement

The NeuralMind project reserves the right to:

- **Request removal** of trademark uses that violate this policy
- **Revoke permission** for permitted uses that become misleading or harmful
- **Pursue legal action** for willful infringement

---

## 7. Third-Party Trademarks in NeuralMind Materials

NeuralMind's own documentation, wiki, and websites regularly name other projects and products (e.g. graphify, Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Headroom). When they do:

1. **Nominative use only** — third-party names identify the third party's own product, accurately and no more than necessary.
2. **No implied endorsement** — comparisons and integration notes must not suggest the third party sponsors or endorses NeuralMind.
3. **Attribution and links** — the first substantive mention of a third-party open-source project in a document links to its canonical home and, where relevant, credits its license and author (see [NOTICE](../NOTICE)).
4. **Accuracy over marketing** — claims about a third-party product (required vs. optional, compatible, benchmarked) must reflect the current release; stale claims are treated as documentation bugs.

All third-party marks remain the property of their respective owners.
