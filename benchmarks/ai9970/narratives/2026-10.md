# 2026-10 — performance record

## A. Site

First month on the record: two of our own repos, measured on 2026-10-22 with NeuralMind v4.20.0 on one dev box. Each has 12 hand-written questions with gold files, so one question moves a rate by a twelfth. Read these as a starting point, not a verdict.

- **rfnode-guide** (618 nodes): the gold file is in the top five for 9 of 12 questions, and first for 8 of those 9. The other 3 are misses outside the top five. Its mean context is 866 tokens, 60.3× fewer than the 52,218 tokens of code the index covers.
- **bible-llm** (1,941 nodes): the gold file is in the top five for 10 of 12 questions but first for only 5. It finds the file; ranking it first is where it loses. Its mean context is 663 tokens, 273.0× fewer than its 180,906 tokens of indexed code.

These are retrieval numbers. Whether an agent then finishes the task is a separate measurement; the one we have, a 20-task pilot that found no difference, is on the [benchmark page](/benchmark/).

## B. LinkedIn

We started a public performance record for NeuralMind: our own repos, re-measured each month on one dev box, with every raw row committed.

Month one (NeuralMind v4.20.0, 12 questions per repo):
• rfnode-guide: the gold file is in the top five for 9 of 12 questions, and first for 8. 60.3× fewer tokens than its indexed code.
• bible-llm: top five for 10 of 12, but first for only 5. 273.0× fewer tokens than its indexed code.

Twelve questions is a small sample, and the page says so beside every rate. Misses sit next to hits, and a run that does worse than the last one stays on the page, highlighted.

The numbers: https://neuralmind.uk/performance/
Run it on your own repo: neuralmind eval .
