## What's New

### E2 — Quality-Weighted Merge Semantics

When two contributors' edges disagree on the same `(source, target)` pair, merges now use quality-weighted resolution instead of last-write-wins.

- **Quality-weighted resolution** — edges with higher activation + recency + low conflict win
- **Decay-on-conflict (reserved)** — losing edges would degrade multiplicatively, not be deleted (team converges, doesn't fork). Currently unused; no caller passes losers to `merge_to_store()`
- **Contest escalation** — both edges weak + close tie → escalates to E3 peer review instead of forcing a low-confidence winner

### E1 — Contribution-Quality Scoring

Scores each contributor's edges on three axes:

- **Reinforcement** — `log1p(activations) / log1p(30)` — saturating function of activation count
- **Recency** — exponential decay over 30 days
- **Conflict penalty** — edges that conflict with others score lower

### C4 — CI-Gated Tuner Promotion

QualityHarness gates tuner promotion in CI. Only candidates that meet faithfulness, fitness, and session-health thresholds auto-promote.

### D3 — Judge Transcripts

Fixture-based benchmark queries with reference answers across 10 languages. Drives RAGAS-axis quality measurement.

### D4 — Per-Language Fixtures

Deterministic fixture loaders per language (Go, Java, Rust, TypeScript). Reproducible benchmarking.
