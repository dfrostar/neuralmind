## Post #2 (TUE) — Reduced Egress + Compliance — CLAUDE-REVISED

**The best AI code intelligence sends the model 40–70× less of your code.**

Most AI coding tools upload your whole codebase to a third-party API to answer one question.

NeuralMind flips that: every query hits your local graph first and surfaces only the ~2% that's actually relevant.

The embeddings are generated and stored on your machine — NeuralMind makes zero external calls of its own.

The result: dramatically less code egress. And for regulated teams, a built-in local audit trail — every query logged with provenance, mapped to NIST AI RMF controls, ready as evidence for your SOC 2 review.

That's why teams in defense, healthcare, and finance are evaluating it.
